Why Water Quality Monitoring Is Essential in Noida

Noida’s industrial growth has resulted in increased effluent discharge from:

  • Chemical and pharmaceutical units
  • Automobile and engineering factories
  • Food & beverage processing plants
  • Textile and dyeing industries
  • Construction and infrastructure sites
  • STPs and ETPs

Untreated or poorly treated wastewater can contaminate groundwater, rivers, and local drains, posing environmental and health risks. Strict monitoring requirements by UPPCB and CPCB ensure industries operate responsibly.

Compliance Framework: CPCB & UPPCB Guidelines

CPCB & UPPCB Directives

Industries in Noida must comply with regulations set by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board (UPPCB).

  • Mandatory installation of OCEMS for Red & Orange category industries
  • Regular reporting of water quality data to CPCB & UPPCB
  • Strict adherence to effluent discharge standards

Parameters Mandated for Monitoring

  • pH
  • COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand)
  • BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand)
  • TSS (Total Suspended Solids)
  • Flow
  • Conductivity
  • Oil & Grease
  • Ammonical Nitrogen

Real-Time Online Monitoring (OCEMS)

UPPCB mandates real-time monitoring with automated data transmission to CPCB/UPPCB servers and zero tolerance for tampered or missing data.

What Is a Water Quality Monitoring System?

A water quality monitoring system continuously measures effluent quality at STP/ETP or discharge points using online analyzers and industrial-grade sensors.

  • Online water quality analyzers (COD, BOD, pH, TSS)
  • Industrial-grade sensors
  • Data loggers with IoT / GPRS communication
  • CPCB & UPPCB server integration

Challenges Faced by Industries in Noida

  • Frequent UPPCB inspections
  • Network and data transmission issues
  • Power and infrastructure limitations
  • Sensor maintenance in harsh environments
