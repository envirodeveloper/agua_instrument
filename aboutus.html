Profile

Agua Instruments Private Limited is headquartered in Mumbai and has a presence in all major cities like Delhi, Kolkata, Bangalore, and Chennai. We are a leading solutions provider and system integrator in the field of Process Analytics, Process Instrumentation, Environment and Emission Monitoring & Automation. We provide process solutions for pressure, level, flow, temperature, analytics, recording, and digital communications. Today, our innovative enterprise caters to the entire water industry, starting from drinking water to sewage and wastewater, and we strive to achieve the same feat in the Emission market also. We serve various industries like Dye & Dye Intermediate, Pulp & Paper, Pesticides, Pharmaceuticals, Tanneries, Distillery, Sugar, Power Plants, Cement, Oil Refineries, Fertilizer, Chloral Alkali Plants, Units, Common Bio-Medical Waste, Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETP), STPs, and Common Hazardous Waste Incinerators, etc.
