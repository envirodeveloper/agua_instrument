Spectrums Instruments is a company dealing in analytical instruments, with partial import of instruments as well. We serve clients across environmental monitoring, air quality, water quality, and emission monitoring sectors.

📋 Role Overview

We're looking for a smart, proactive Receptionist cum Admin cum Telecaller to manage office support activities. This is a desk-based, back-office role — no walk-in customer handling required.

✅ Key Responsibilities

  • Admin Support: Maintain files, perform data entry, assist with documentation, and manage office supplies.
  • Phone & Email: Manage incoming and outgoing calls and emails; coordinate with vendors and clients.
  • Telecalling: Handle follow-ups and basic inquiries over the phone professionally.

  • Freshers are welcome; 1–3 years experience in admin or telecalling is a plus.

  • Good phone etiquette and the ability to multitask efficiently.
