PC-300A Series pH/ORP Transmitter
Easy & reliable wastewater pH/ORP control

The PC-300A series pH/ORP transmitter provides easy and reliable operation and performance, especially suitable for wastewater application. PC-320A provides Hi1, Hi2, Lo1, Lo2 four relays control for special needs in controlling devices. PH-300T acts as an accessorial field transmitter to extend cable transmission up to 100m for synchronized field display and calibration work

Features:

  • pH / ORP measurement
  • Intuitive tuning-knob calibration and relay setting, easy for weekly wastewater calibration work
  • Enhanced standby function protects from inadvertent operation
  • Two(PC-310A) or four(PC-320A) ON/OFF contact outputs
  • Two/three wires distribution design, possible for connecting solution
  • ground to reduce filed signal interference LED indication alarm
