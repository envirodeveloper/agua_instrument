EC-410/EC-430 ConduCtiVity Monitor
Compact and Pragmatic

The EC-410 & EC-430 conductivity monitors utilize microprocessor technology to provide enhanced capabilities with economical price. The compact design fits in small space installation. The EC-410 features for water quality monitoring and the EC-430 offers one relay output to sufficiently activate your process system.

Features:

  • 48 x 96 mm Microprocessor pH/ORP Monitor & Controller Sensor
  • Compatible with both conductivity and resistivity measurement
  • Compatible with 2-Electrode or 4-Electrode sensors
  • Automatic temperature compensation
  • Compatible with Pt-1000 and NTC 30K temperature probe Linear and non-linear temperature coefficient
  • Isolated current output DC 4~20mA
  • Gradually continuous sample measurements average function to enhance stability
  • EC-430 offers one relay contact output, one set point with programmable hysteresis function

    • Application

    • RO system
    • Boiler feed water
    • Cooling water
    • Ultra-pure water
    • Industrial process
    • Water treatment
