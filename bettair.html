The Device

A node built for the real world

The Bettair® node is a compact, dome-shaped sensor hub designed for urban environments. Its patented sensing cartridge can have all sensors replaced without specialist tools — and requires no on-site calibration.

💧
IP65 Rated Dust-tight & water-resistant for any outdoor environment
🔄
Swappable Cartridge Replace all sensors when required — no in-situ calibration needed
🌡️
Wide Operating Range Full accuracy from −10 °C to 40 °C; up to 95% non-condensing humidity
📡
Dual Connectivity LoRaWAN and 4G (LTE) for flexible network deployment

Why It Matters

How pollution affects the human body

Outdoor air pollution kills 4.5 million people annually — mostly in cities. Different pollutants target different organs and systems.

🧠
Particulate Matter (PM)
Central Nervous System
Fine particles cross the blood-brain barrier, linked to cognitive decline and neurological damage.
🫀
O₃, PM
Heart Disease
Ozone and particulates increase risk of cardiovascular disease and heart attacks.
🫁
Particulate Matter (PM)
Lungs & Respiratory System
Causes asthma, reduced breathing capacity, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
🩸
NO₂
Liver, Spleen & Blood
Nitrogen dioxide disrupts the oxygen-carrying capacity of red blood cells.
👁️
O₃, PM, NO₂, SO₂, NH₃
Eyes, Nose & Throat
Multiple pollutants cause irritation of mucous membranes and upper respiratory tract.
🧬
Particulate Matter (PM)
Reproductive System
Long-term PM exposure is associated with hormonal disruption and adverse birth outcomes.
