Why It Matters

How air pollution affects the human body

Outdoor air pollution kills 4.5 million people annually — mostly in cities. Understanding which pollutants affect which organs is the first step to taking action.

🧠
Particulate Matter (PM)
Central Nervous System
Fine particles cross the blood-brain barrier, linked to cognitive decline and neurological damage.
🫀
O₃, PM
Heart Disease
Ozone and particulates increase risk of cardiovascular disease and heart attacks.
🫁
Particulate Matter (PM)
Lungs & Respiratory System
Causes asthma, reduced breathing capacity, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
🩸
NO₂
Liver, Spleen & Blood
Nitrogen dioxide disrupts the oxygen-carrying capacity of red blood cells.
👁️
O₃, PM, NO₂, SO₂, NH₃
Eyes, Nose & Throat
Multiple pollutants cause irritation of mucous membranes and the upper respiratory tract.
🧬
Particulate Matter (PM)
Reproductive System
Long-term PM exposure is associated with hormonal disruption and adverse birth outcomes.
