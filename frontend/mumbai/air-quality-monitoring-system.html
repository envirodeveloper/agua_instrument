Air Quality Monitoring System in Mumbai (AQMS)

Mumbai — India's financial capital and one of the world's most densely populated cities — faces significant air quality challenges. A unique combination of vehicular exhaust, industrial emissions from MIDC clusters, construction dust, and sea-salt aerosols creates a complex pollution profile that demands continuous, precise air quality monitoring. Seasonal shifts driven by the Arabian Sea's monsoon patterns further alter pollutant dispersion, making real-time data essential for decision-makers, industries, and urban planners.

The Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) mandates continuous ambient air quality monitoring for industries, construction projects, and urban zones across Greater Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and Thane. Agua Instruments provides MPCB-compliant AQMS solutions that integrate seamlessly with CPCB's Central Pollution Control Board data portals, ensuring industries meet their statutory reporting obligations without disruption.

Key pollutants monitored in Mumbai include Suspended Particulate Matter (SPM), PM2.5, PM10, Sulphur Dioxide (SO₂), Nitrogen Oxides (NOₓ), Carbon Monoxide (CO), and Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) — all critical indicators in a megacity environment where sources are varied and overlapping.

From Dharavi and BKC to Navi Mumbai's industrial belt and the Eastern Express Highway corridor, our monitoring network design covers Mumbai's highest-risk zones: traffic hotspots, industrial peripheries, residential dense areas, and coastal stations where sea-spray interacts with combustion pollutants.

Monitoring sites in Mumbai are strategically chosen near Chembur's industrial corridor, Andheri's transport hubs, Lower Parel's commercial belt, and MIDC estates in Thane, Ambernath, and Taloja — areas where pollution events are frequent and regulatory scrutiny is highest.

Our advanced air quality monitoring sensors and cloud-connected data platforms provide accurate, continuous data collection, enabling Mumbai's industries and civic bodies to make evidence-based decisions for a cleaner, healthier city.

Need for AQMS in Mumbai

Mumbai consistently ranks among India's most polluted cities, with PM2.5 levels frequently exceeding NAAQS limits during winter months and post-monsoon construction seasons. Our comprehensive ambient air quality monitoring stations deploy sensor-based analyzer modules calibrated with Standard Reference Materials, offering Mumbai's industries a reliable, MPCB-accepted data stream for compliance reporting and operational decision-making.

With our continuous AQMS, users in Mumbai can simultaneously track gas concentrations, particulate matter levels, and environmental parameters in real time.
