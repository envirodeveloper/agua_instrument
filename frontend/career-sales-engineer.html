About the Role

We are seeking enthusiastic, self-motivated and career-oriented candidates with a background in BE, MBA, EXTC, Instrumentation & Control Engineering, B.Sc. Responsibilities

What You'll Do

  • Identify and approach potential customers in the Chemical, Pharmaceutical, Petrochemical and other industrial sectors.
  • Generate new business leads through telephone calls, emails, networking and other communication channels.
  • Identify new business opportunities and develop potential customer relationships for Water & Gas Analyzer and Analytical Instrumentation solutions.
  • Introduce and promote the company's Water & Gas Analyzers and analytical instruments to prospective customers.
  • Understand customer requirements and recommend suitable products and technical solutions.
  • Conduct product presentations, demonstrations and customer meetings to communicate product features and applications.
  • Manage sales enquiries, prepare quotations and follow up on business opportunities to support order conversion.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with existing and prospective customers.
  • Coordinate with technical teams to provide effective pre-sales and post-sales support.
  • Explore new markets and contribute towards expanding the company's customer and dealer network.
  • Conduct market research and stay updated on industry developments and competitor activities.
  • Maintain sales records and prepare regular business development reports.

What We're Looking For

  • BE, MBA, EXTC, Instrumentation & Control Engineering, B.Sc. Chemistry or Chemical Engineering degree from a recognised institution. Diploma holders are also welcome to apply.

  • 1–3 years of experience in sales, marketing or business development.
  • Fresh graduates with a strong interest in technical sales are encouraged to apply.
  • Interest in analytical instrumentation, Water & Gas Analyzers and industrial monitoring solutions.
  • Good communication, interpersonal and presentation skills.
  • Ability to understand customer requirements and communicate technical product information effectively.
  • Strong relationship-building, coordination and negotiation skills.
  • Self-motivated, proactive and target-oriented approach.

  • Willingness to travel for customer visits and business development activities, when required.
