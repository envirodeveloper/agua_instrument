8-241 Series ConduCtiVity Sensor
For medium to high conductivity solutions

The 8-241 series conductivity sensors apply the state of art 4-Electrode measurement principle to accurately measure medium to high conductivity solutions. Its modular cable and sensor design provides the most convenience of sensor re-installation. The improved high temperature tolerance feature makes the 8-241 series sensors more durable in even more applications. The 8-241-01 sensor is a HF resistant sensor for some of electronics industry process application.
