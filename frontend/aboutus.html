Our Profile

Who We Are

Agua Instruments Private Limited is headquartered in Mumbai with a presence in all major cities like Delhi, Kolkata, Bangalore, and Chennai.

We are a leading solutions provider and system integrator in the field of Process Analytics, Process Instrumentation, Environment and Emission Monitoring & Automation. We provide process solutions for pressure, level, flow, temperature, analytics, recording, and digital communications.

Today, our innovative enterprise caters to the entire water industry — from drinking water to sewage and wastewater — and we strive to achieve the same feat in the Emission market.
