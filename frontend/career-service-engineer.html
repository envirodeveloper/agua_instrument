Overview

About the Role

Agua Instruments Pvt. Ltd. is looking for enthusiastic and technically inclined engineering graduates who are interested in building a career in Analytical Instrumentation, Industrial Automation and Field Service Engineering.

The selected candidates will receive hands-on training and practical field exposure in the installation, commissioning, calibration, testing, troubleshooting, maintenance and servicing of analytical instruments and monitoring systems. The role provides exposure to Water Quality Monitoring Analyzers, Gas Analyzers, Air Quality Analyzers and other analytical instrumentation used in industrial applications.

Responsibilities

What You'll Do

Field Service & Technical Support

  • Assist in installation, testing, commissioning, start-up and maintenance of analytical instruments and monitoring systems.
  • Attend customer service calls and assist in fault finding, troubleshooting, repair and corrective actions.
  • Perform instrument testing and calibration as per standard procedures.
  • Assist senior engineers during field installations, commissioning and troubleshooting.
  • Identify technical issues and assist in determining the root cause and appropriate solution.
  • Carry out preventive and corrective maintenance of instruments and associated equipment.

Customer Support & Training

  • Provide technical assistance to customers during installation, operation and troubleshooting.
  • Assist in training customers' technical personnel in operation, maintenance and basic troubleshooting.
  • Respond to customer complaints and provide timely technical support.
  • Develop and maintain professional relationships with customers.
  • Provide customers with regular updates and feedback on service requests.

Documentation & Reporting

  • Prepare and maintain service reports for AMC, warranty and out-of-warranty visits.
  • Maintain installation, commissioning, calibration, testing and troubleshooting records.
  • Maintain and update product manuals and technical documentation.
  • Report recurring technical issues and customer complaints to management for corrective action.

Sales & Business Support

  • Work with the Sales Team to support service sales and spare/parts sales.
  • Provide technical inputs during customer discussions and product demonstrations.
  • Identify potential service and spare-parts requirements at customer sites.
  • Support customer training and technical demonstrations.

Technical Skills

Skills You'll Use

Electronics & PCB

  • Basic knowledge of resistors, capacitors, transistors, diodes, relays, ICs and other electronic components.
  • Basic hands-on knowledge of SMD soldering and component replacement.
  • Ability to perform PCB track tracing and basic circuit analysis.
  • Ability to understand basic electrical/electronic circuit and wiring diagrams.
  • Familiarity with digital multimeter, soldering station, DC power supply, oscilloscope and other testing tools.

Communication & Networking

  • Good understanding of RS-485 communication.
  • Basic knowledge of RS-232, Modbus and HART protocols.
  • Basic understanding of IoT protocols such as MQTT, CoAP and HTTP.
  • Basic knowledge of LAN, IP addressing, network configuration, connectivity testing and troubleshooting.

Instrumentation

  • Basic knowledge of instrument calibration, testing and troubleshooting.
  • Familiarity with peristaltic pumps, flow meters, 3-way control valves and solenoid valves.
  • Basic understanding of analytical instruments and industrial measurement systems will be an added advantage.
