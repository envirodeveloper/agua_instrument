Water Quality Monitoring System in Mumbai: CPCB & MPCB Compliance Guide

Mumbai, India’s financial and industrial capital, faces serious challenges related to industrial effluent discharge, coastal water pollution, and regulatory compliance. With strict enforcement by CPCB and MPCB, industries must adopt real-time water quality monitoring systems.

This guide explains CPCB & MPCB compliance requirements, the importance of online water quality monitoring, and how Agua Instruments helps Mumbai industries meet regulatory norms seamlessly.

Why Water Quality Monitoring Is Critical in Mumbai

Mumbai hosts a wide range of industries that generate wastewater requiring proper treatment through STPs and ETPs before discharge.

  • Chemical & petrochemical units
  • Pharmaceutical manufacturing
  • Food & beverage processing
  • Textile & dyeing units
  • Power plants and infrastructure projects

Due to coastal proximity, dense population, and sensitive ecosystems like creeks and mangroves, MPCB enforces stringent monitoring norms.

CPCB & MPCB Guidelines for Water Quality Monitoring

1. Mandatory OCEMS Installation

  • Required for Red and Orange category industries
  • Continuous real-time monitoring
  • Automatic data transmission to CPCB & MPCB servers
  • Tamper-proof analyzers and data loggers

2. Parameters Mandated

  • pH
  • BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand)
  • COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand)
  • TSS (Total Suspended Solids)
  • Flow
  • Ammonical Nitrogen
  • Oil & Grease
  • Conductivity

3. Data Transmission & Security

  • Real-time GPRS / LAN / IoT communication
  • Encrypted, secure data protocols
  • No manual data intervention allowed

What Is an Online Water Quality Monitoring System?

An Online Water Quality Monitoring System continuously measures effluent parameters using online analyzers installed at discharge and treatment outlets.

  • Online COD, BOD, pH & TSS analyzers
  • Industrial-grade water quality sensors
  • CPCB-compatible data logger
  • IoT communication module
  • CPCB & MPCB server integration

Compliance Challenges for Mumbai Industries

  • Space constraints in STP/ETP layouts
  • Server integration issues
