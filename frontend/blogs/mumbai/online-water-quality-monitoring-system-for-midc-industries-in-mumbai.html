Online Water Quality Monitoring System for MIDC Industries in Mumbai

The Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation (MIDC) zones in Mumbai host a wide range of industries, including chemical, pharmaceutical, engineering, textile, food processing, and manufacturing units.

Due to the high density of industrial activity, MIDC areas are under strict environmental surveillance by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB).

To prevent water pollution and ensure regulatory compliance, online water quality monitoring systems have become mandatory for many MIDC industries in Mumbai.

This article explains the importance, regulatory requirements, and benefits of online water quality monitoring systems for MIDC industries, and how Agua Instruments helps industries achieve seamless, audit-ready compliance.

Why MIDC Industries in Mumbai Need Online Water Quality Monitoring

  • High effluent generation
  • Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETPs)
  • Discharge into municipal drains, creeks, or marine outlets

Even one non-compliant unit can impact the entire MIDC cluster, resulting in stricter inspections, collective penalties, or shutdowns. Online monitoring ensures compliance at the individual unit level.

CPCB & MPCB Requirements for MIDC Industries

1. Mandatory OCEMS Installation

  • Continuous monitoring of effluent parameters
  • Real-time data transmission to CPCB & MPCB servers
  • Tamper-proof analyzers and data loggers

2. Parameters to Be Monitored

  • pH
  • COD
  • BOD
  • TSS
  • Flow
  • Conductivity
  • Oil & Grease
  • Ammonical Nitrogen

These parameters directly reflect ETP performance and treated effluent quality.

What Is an Online Water Quality Monitoring System?

An online water quality monitoring system continuously measures effluent parameters using online analyzers and industrial-grade sensors, eliminating manual sampling.

  • Online water analyzers (COD, BOD, pH, TSS, etc.)
  • Industrial-grade sensors
  • Secure data logger
  • IoT / GPRS communication module
  • CPCB & MPCB server integration

Challenges Faced by MIDC Industries in Mumbai

  • Frequent MPCB inspections
  • Data transmission and system uptime issues
