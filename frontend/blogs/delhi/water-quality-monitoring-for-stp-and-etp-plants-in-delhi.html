Water Quality Monitoring for STP & ETP Plants in Delhi (CPCB & DPCC Compliance)

Delhi generates enormous volumes of domestic sewage and industrial wastewater, making Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) and Effluent Treatment Plants (ETPs) critical for protecting the city’s water resources.

To ensure treated wastewater meets prescribed discharge standards and to safeguard the Yamuna River and groundwater, CPCB and DPCC mandate real-time water quality monitoring for STP and ETP plants across Delhi.

This blog explains the importance of water quality monitoring for STP & ETP plants in Delhi, key regulatory requirements, and how Agua Instruments supports reliable, regulation-ready compliance.

Why STP & ETP Monitoring Is Critical in Delhi

  • High hydraulic and organic loading
  • Limited land availability in urban areas
  • Aging drainage and sewer infrastructure
  • Strict CPCB & DPCC enforcement

Any failure in treatment efficiency can directly impact the Yamuna River, groundwater safety, and public health. Hence, continuous and transparent monitoring is mandatory.

CPCB & DPCC Requirements for STP & ETP Monitoring

1. Mandatory Online Monitoring (OCEMS)

  • Industrial ETPs
  • Large STPs operated by industries, hospitals, hotels, and commercial complexes
  • Direct real-time data transmission to CPCB & DPCC servers

2. Key Parameters for STP & ETP Plants

  • pH
  • BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand)
  • COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand)
  • TSS (Total Suspended Solids)
  • Flow
  • Conductivity
  • Ammonical Nitrogen
  • Oil & Grease

Monitoring these parameters ensures treatment performance, discharge compliance, and environmental protection.
