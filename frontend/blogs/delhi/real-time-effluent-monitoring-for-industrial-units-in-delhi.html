Real-Time Effluent Monitoring for Industrial Units in Delhi

Delhi, one of India’s most densely populated and industrially active regions, faces increasing pressure on its rivers, groundwater, and urban environment.

To combat pollution and ensure sustainable industrial practices, CPCB and DPCC have mandated real-time effluent monitoring for many industrial units.

This blog highlights the importance of real-time effluent monitoring in Delhi, outlines regulatory requirements, and explains how Agua Instruments helps industries achieve continuous compliance while protecting the environment.

Why Real-Time Effluent Monitoring Is Critical in Delhi

  • Strict DPCC consent conditions
  • Frequent inspections and audits
  • Zero tolerance for pollution violations

Traditional manual sampling and periodic testing are no longer sufficient. Real-time effluent monitoring provides continuous visibility, regulatory assurance, and operational efficiency.

What Is Real-Time Effluent Monitoring?

Real-time effluent monitoring involves continuous measurement of wastewater quality using online analyzers and sensors installed at industrial discharge points.

Key Features

  • 24×7 continuous monitoring
  • Automatic data logging and reporting
  • Secure transmission to CPCB & DPCC servers
  • Tamper-proof and reliable data handling

This forms the core of Online Continuous Effluent Monitoring Systems (OCEMS), now mandatory for Red and Orange category industries in Delhi.

CPCB & DPCC Requirements for Industrial Units in Delhi

1. Mandatory OCEMS Installation

  • Continuous monitoring of effluent parameters
  • Real-time data access for regulators
  • No data gaps or manipulation

2. Key Parameters Monitored in Real Time

  • pH
  • COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand)
  • BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand)
  • TSS (Total Suspended Solids)
  • Flow
  • Conductivity
  • Oil & Grease
  • Ammonical Nitrogen

These parameters reflect treatment efficiency and ensure safe discharge into Delhi’s rivers and drains.

3. Data Transmission & System Uptime

  • Secure GPRS / LAN / IoT connectivity
  • Uninterrupted power supply
  • 24×7 system availability

Any disruption in monitoring is treated as non-compliance by DPCC.
