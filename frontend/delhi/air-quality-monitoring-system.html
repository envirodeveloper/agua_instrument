Air Quality Monitoring System in Delhi NCR (AQMS)

Delhi NCR is consistently ranked among the world's most polluted urban regions, with air quality deteriorating sharply during winter months when a combination of stubble burning from neighbouring Punjab and Haryana, vehicular exhaust from over 11 million registered vehicles, industrial emissions from Faridabad, Ghaziabad, and Noida, and low wind speeds trap pollutants in a dense smog layer over the National Capital Territory. PM2.5 levels during peak episodes routinely exceed 500 μg/m³ — more than 20 times the WHO guideline — making continuous, precise air quality monitoring not just a regulatory requirement but a public health necessity.

The Delhi Pollution Control Committee (DPCC) and the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) mandate continuous ambient air quality and emission monitoring for industries, construction sites, and major infrastructure projects across the NCR. Agua Instruments provides DPCC- and CPCB-compliant AQMS solutions with direct data transmission to regulatory servers — ensuring industries across Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad, and Ghaziabad meet their statutory obligations without operational disruption.

Key pollutants monitored across Delhi NCR include PM2.5, PM10, Total Suspended Particulates (TSP), Sulphur Dioxide (SO₂), Nitrogen Oxides (NOₓ), Carbon Monoxide (CO), Ozone (O₃), and Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) — the full spectrum required under NAAQS and the Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) implemented by the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM).

Monitoring sites across Delhi NCR must account for diverse emission profiles: traffic-heavy corridors like NH-48, Ring Road, and Mathura Road; industrial clusters in Okhla, Wazirpur, and Bawana; construction hotspots in Aerocity and Dwarka Expressway; and sensitive receptor zones near schools, hospitals, and residential areas in Rohini, Dwarka, and South Delhi.

Delhi's Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) triggers emergency restrictions based on real-time AQI data — making accurate, continuous monitoring critical for industries that need to adapt operations as air quality thresholds are crossed. Our cloud-connected AQMS provides live GRAP-stage alerts, enabling proactive compliance management.

Our advanced air quality monitoring sensors deliver high-accuracy, continuous data for Delhi's complex multi-source pollution environment, supporting DPCC compliance, CAQM reporting, and smart city air quality management initiatives across the National Capital Region.

Need for AQMS in Delhi NCR

Delhi's Air Quality Index (AQI) enters the "Severe" and "Severe+" categories for extended periods between October and February, triggering GRAP Stage III and IV restrictions that directly impact industrial operations. DPCC's mandatory online monitoring requirement covers all industrial units operating under the Red and Orange categories. Our ambient air quality monitoring stations deploy sensor-based analyzer modules calibrated with Standard Reference Materials — providing a reliable, DPCC-accepted data stream for statutory reporting and operational decision-making across the NCR.

With our continuous AQMS, Delhi NCR industries can simultaneously monitor gas concentrations, particulate matter, and meteorological parameters in real time. Data is accessible remotely via PC, tablet, or smartphone — essential for environmental officers and plant managers overseeing multiple facilities across Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, and Faridabad simultaneously.
