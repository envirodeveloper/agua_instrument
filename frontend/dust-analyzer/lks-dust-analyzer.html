About the D10.3

The D10.3 is a measuring device that combines the advantages of transmittance and forward scattered light — ideal for very low to high dust concentrations. Its automatic self-alignment optimises the position of the measuring beam and prevents incorrect measurements.

The device also features automatic checks of zero and reference points, contamination monitoring, and compensation on both sender and receiver sides. A light emitting diode serves as the light source, ensuring long service life and consistent output.

Measuring Principle

The D10.3 is a type-approved measuring device that combines the advantages of the measuring principles of transmittance and scattered light forward. It is therefore ideal for measurement of very low to high dust concentrations.

The automatic self-alignment optimises the position of the measuring beam and prevents incorrect measurements. An automatic check of zero and reference points, as well as contamination monitoring, are included as standard.
